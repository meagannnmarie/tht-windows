About Our Founder

Why Mike Built Home Team

Mike Martin didn't start Home Team to build another roofing company. He started it because he knew homeowners deserved a better experience.

Before founding Home Team, Mike worked as an insurance adjuster, where he saw firsthand how overwhelming the repair process could become for homeowners — especially when major damage, insurance claims, and contractors all collided at once. That experience gave him a rare perspective on what homeowners actually needed: straight answers, clear communication, quality work, and a team willing to take ownership from start to finish.

He saw an opportunity to do it differently.

Home Team was built around a higher standard: communicate clearly, show up when you say you will, take pride in the craftsmanship, and stand behind the work long after the project is complete.

That philosophy turned one roofing company into a trusted Texas home improvement team with more than 45,000 installations completed.

The company has grown. The standard hasn't.

"When someone trusts us with their home, they're trusting our entire team to do things the right way. That responsibility means everything to me."

Today, Mike still leads Home Team by the same principle it was built on: take care of people, build something you're proud to put your name on, and earn the customer's trust every step of the way.

Our Mission

The Mission of Home Team Roofing

Our mission is simple: to take care of people, protect their homes, and deliver work we are proud to put the Home Team name behind.

We believe homeowners deserve more than a contractor. They deserve a team that communicates clearly, shows up when promised, respects their property, stands behind its workmanship, and treats every project with the same care we would expect for our own families.

Home Team was built on the belief that strong companies are created through strong relationships, honest service, skilled craftsmanship, and accountability at every level. From the first conversation to the final walkthrough, our goal is to make every homeowner feel informed, respected, and confident in the team they chose.

As we continue to grow across Texas, our commitment remains the same: never sacrifice quality for growth, never lose sight of the people we serve, and never compromise the standards attached to the Home Team name.

"One team. One standard. Take care of people and do the job right."
