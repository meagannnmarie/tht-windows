Storm Damage Support

When the Storm Passes, Home Team Is Ready.

Houston weather can move fast. When it does, Home Team can inspect your roof, document visible damage, walk you through your options and support you throughout the restoration process.

01
Schedule an Inspection

Contact Home Team to arrange a free post-storm roof inspection at your earliest convenience.

02
Assess and Document Damage

Our team walks your roof and records all visible storm-related damage with photos and a written assessment.

03
Review Roofing Recommendations

We present your options clearly — repair or full replacement — and explain what each approach involves and why.

04
Coordinate the Restoration Process

We support you through the insurance process and work to coordinate restoration as smoothly as possible.

05
Install and Complete the Final Walkthrough

Your roof is restored by an experienced crew. We complete a final walkthrough together before considering the project done.
